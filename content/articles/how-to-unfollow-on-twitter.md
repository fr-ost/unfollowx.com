Unfollowing someone on Twitter (X) takes two taps once you know where the button is. This guide shows every way to unfollow on the web, on iPhone and on Android, how to see the full list of accounts you follow, and what actually changes when you unfollow someone. If you need to remove more than a handful of accounts, skip to [unfollowing hundreds at once](#need-to-unfollow-hundreds-at-once).

> [!NOTE] X redesigns its apps from time to time, so a button may look slightly different from the description here. The labels — **Following**, **Unfollow** and the **⋯** (More) menu — have stayed the same for years.

## How to unfollow someone on Twitter (X) on the web

These steps work on x.com and twitter.com in any desktop browser.

### From their profile

1. Go to the profile of the account you want to unfollow.
2. Find the **Following** button next to their name. When you hover over it, it turns red and reads **Unfollow**.
3. Click it. If X asks you to confirm, click **Unfollow** again.

The button changes back to **Follow**, which means you no longer follow that account.

### From one of their posts

1. On any post from that account, click the **⋯** (More) icon in the top-right corner of the post.
2. Choose **Unfollow @handle**.

This is the quickest way when an account you no longer want shows up in your timeline.

### From your Following list

1. Click **Profile** in the left-hand menu.
2. Click the **Following** count under your bio. The address is `x.com/yourhandle/following`.
3. Next to any account, hover over **Following** and click **Unfollow**, then confirm if asked.

This is the best place to unfollow several accounts in a row, because you stay on the same page.

## How to unfollow on Twitter (X) on iPhone

In the X app for iPhone and iPad:

1. Open the profile of the account you want to unfollow (tap their name or photo on any post, or search for them).
2. Tap the **Following** button.
3. Tap **Unfollow** in the confirmation that appears.

To unfollow from a post, tap the **⋯** icon on the post and choose **Unfollow @handle**. To work through your whole list, tap your profile picture, open your **Profile**, tap **Following** and use the **Following** button next to each account.

## How to unfollow on Twitter (X) on Android

The Android app works the same way:

1. Open the account’s profile.
2. Tap **Following**.
3. Confirm with **Unfollow**.

From a post, tap the **⋯** icon and choose **Unfollow @handle**. Your Following list is under your profile picture → **Profile** → **Following**.

## How to see who you follow on Twitter (X)

Every account you follow is listed on your Following page:

- **Web:** Profile → **Following**, or go straight to `x.com/yourhandle/following`.
- **iPhone and Android:** tap your profile picture → **Profile** → **Following**.

The list shows the most recently followed accounts first. A **Follows you** label appears next to accounts that follow you back — useful if you want to unfollow people who don’t. For long lists, scrolling and checking labels one by one gets slow; our guide on [how to see who doesn’t follow you back](/blog/how-to-see-who-doesnt-follow-you-back-on-x) covers faster methods, including a [free non-followers checker](/twitter-non-followers-checker).

## What happens when you unfollow someone

- **They aren’t notified.** X doesn’t send a notification when someone unfollows them. They can only notice by checking their followers or by using a follower tracker.
- **Their posts leave your Following timeline.** Posts from that account may still appear in **For you** if X’s recommendations pick them, and you can still visit their profile.
- **Private accounts are different.** If the account is protected, you lose access to their posts. Following them again sends a new follow request they have to approve.
- **Nothing else is removed.** Your past likes, replies and messages stay as they are, and they still follow you unless they choose to unfollow too.

If you only want to stop seeing someone’s posts without them noticing a change in your follow count, muting is an option — see [mute vs unfollow vs block on X](/blog/mute-vs-unfollow-vs-block-on-x).

## How to undo an unfollow

Unfollowed someone by accident? Open their profile and tap **Follow** again. If you don’t remember the handle, check your recent search history or a post they wrote. Our guide on [recovering from an accidental unfollow](/blog/how-to-recover-from-an-accidental-unfollow-on-x) covers what the other person sees and how to avoid mistakes during a bigger cleanup.

## Quick answers

**Can you unfollow on Twitter without the person knowing?** X doesn’t notify anyone when they’re unfollowed, but they can see it if they check their followers list or use a tracking tool.

**Is there an “unfollow all” button on X?** No. X has no built-in bulk unfollow. You can unfollow one account at a time, or use a tool — see [how to unfollow everyone on Twitter (X) at once](/blog/how-to-unfollow-everyone-on-x).

**Why does the Unfollow button not work?** Refresh the page or restart the app, check your connection, and try again later. If X is limiting your account’s actions, wait before trying again.

See who follows you back

Scan the accounts you follow on X to see mutuals and accounts that don’t follow you back. Review the results in a dashboard, search names and handles, and select individual accounts or a larger group. You stay in control of the final selection.

The scan reads your following list from X in your signed-in browser session. It can also identify accounts without a blue check and, when X makes the data available, show the date of an account’s latest post.

Keep track of follower changes

Save a followers snapshot, then check again later to compare. The Followers view can show accounts that have left since your previous complete check, new followers and people who follow you but whom you don’t follow back. The extension avoids comparing an incomplete list, so a partial result is not treated as a confirmed change.

Search and filter before you decide

Search by name, handle or bio. Sort and filter by follow-back status, activity, verification, profile photo, post count, follower count, following ratio, account age, keywords, and writing system. Availability depends on the profile information X exposes in your session.

Protect accounts you want to keep

Add individual accounts to your whitelist. You can also set Keep rules for verified or private accounts, accounts above a follower threshold, accounts with chosen words in their profile, and accounts you followed recently. These protections apply to bulk cleanup modes.

Review history and follow back

See accounts the extension has followed or unfollowed and when the action occurred. Follow again from your history if you change your mind. The Followers view can also help you choose new followers to follow back.

Save groups and use CSV

Save selected accounts as a named group to revisit later. Import a list of handles from a CSV or pasted text to target specific accounts. Export lists, selections, follower changes or action history to CSV for your own records.

Set a pace that works for you

Choose a speed preset or customize the delay between actions, breaks and a rolling daily limit. Pause, resume or stop a run. The extension can back off if X asks you to slow down and stop if X requires an account check. These controls reduce aggressive activity but cannot guarantee that X will never rate-limit or restrict an account.

Designed for your browser

UnfollowX Pro is a Chrome extension for x.com and twitter.com. It supports multiple X accounts, with scans, whitelist, history, groups and counters kept separately per account on your device. The interface includes English, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Hindi, Indonesian, Turkish and Arabic, plus light and dark themes.

UnfollowX Pro is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by X Corp. or Twitter. X can change its site and policies at any time.
